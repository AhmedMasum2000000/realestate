Billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh

Billboard advertising in Bangladesh costs about 100 to 600 BDT per minute on an LED billboard, or about 500 to 3,000 BDT per square foot to rent a printed static billboard. The final price depends on the location, how often your ad plays, the campaign length and how many screens you book.

What changes the price

  • Location: busy Dhaka junctions such as Gulshan, Banani and Airport Road cost the most.
  • Slots per hour: LED screens share time between advertisers; more plays means more people see you.
  • Length: a month is priced better per day than a one-week burst.
  • Number of screens: packages across one area or several cities are priced as a package.
  • Creative: bringing a finished file costs nothing; design and animation are quoted separately.

Questions about price

How much does billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh?

An LED billboard costs about 100 to 600 BDT per minute of airtime, depending on the screen, slots per hour and dates. A printed static billboard is rented at about 500 to 3,000 BDT per square foot, plus printing and installation. Your written quote confirms the current rate.

What is the billboard rent in Dhaka?

Dhaka is the most expensive market, because its junctions carry the most traffic. Gulshan, Banani, Airport Road and Mohakhali sit at the top of the 100 to 600 BDT per minute LED range; quieter roads cost less. Compare the Dhaka locations.

Is an LED billboard cheaper than a static billboard?

For a short campaign, usually yes: there is no printing or installation, you can start within days and change the ad mid-campaign. For a long booking on one site, a static billboard can cost less per day, but you share no time with other advertisers.

What is the cheapest way to start?

Book one well-placed LED screen for a short burst, or a few slots per hour across two or three screens in the same area. Ask for a package: several screens in one area are priced better than single bookings.

Do prices include the design of the ad?

Airtime and design are quoted separately. Sending a finished video or image costs nothing extra; we can also make it for you.

Is VAT included?

Your written quote states the taxes that apply. Ask for the total including VAT when you compare offers.
