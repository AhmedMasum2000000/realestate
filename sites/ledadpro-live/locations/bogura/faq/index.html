How much does LED billboard advertising cost in Bogura?

It depends on the screen, the number of slots per hour and your dates. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts LED billboard time from about 100 to 600 BDT per minute; the written quote confirms the current rate. Read the Bogura cost guide.

Which Bogura screen should I choose?

Start with the direction your audience travels. Each screen faces a specific road or junction, for example Police Plaza, Bogura (facing Circuit House). Compare the facing of each screen on the audience and routes page.

How quickly can my ad run on a Bogura screen?

Once the dates, screens and artwork are confirmed in writing, an LED campaign can usually start within days, because there is nothing to print or install. Availability is confirmed when you enquire.

What file format do LED billboards need?

Ask for the exact pixel size and orientation of each screen in your proposal. Most LED screens play a short video (MP4) or still image (JPG/PNG) made to that size, with large text and strong contrast so it reads from the road.

Can I book several Bogura screens together?

Yes. You can combine screens in Bogura or add nearby areas such as Rajshahi, Rangpur, Airport. A package is priced by its screens and slots, not as one screen.

Do you handle permissions and installation?

For LED AD PRO’s own screens the placement is already in place, so you book airtime only. For new billboards or custom LED displays, we discuss permissions, design and installation as part of the written proposal.
