How to compare Mohakhali screens

  • Facing: a screen is seen by traffic coming towards it, so the facing tells you which journey it reaches.
  • Size and height: ask for the pixel size and the height above the road; it decides how much text people can read.
  • Slots: LED screens share time between advertisers. Ask how many slots per hour your ad gets.
  • Recent photo: ask for a current photo or video of the screen running, as surroundings change.
