LED billboard advertising cost in Cox's Bazar

OverviewLED screensCostAudience & routesPlanningFAQ

LED billboard advertising in Cox's Bazar is priced by screen, airtime and dates. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts LED billboard time from about 100 to 600 BDT per minute, with premium junctions at the higher end. Your written quote confirms the current rate for the screens you choose.

Static billboards

Printed static billboards are priced differently: rent per square foot per month, plus printing and installation. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts regular billboard rent from about 500 to 3,000 BDT per square foot, depending on location and size.
