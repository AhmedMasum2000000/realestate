Match the screen to your goal

  • Shops and restaurants in Gulshan: choose the screen on the road your customers use to reach you, and show the turning or landmark.
  • Brand awareness: combine several facings so you meet traffic in both directions.
  • Launches and events: run more slots for a short, intense burst before the date.
  • City-wide reach: add nearby areas: Banani, Mohakhali, Tejgaon.
