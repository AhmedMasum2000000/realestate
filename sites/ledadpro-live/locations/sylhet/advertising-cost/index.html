LED billboard advertising cost in Sylhet

OverviewLED screensCostAudience & routesPlanningFAQ

LED billboard advertising in Sylhet is priced by screen, airtime and dates. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts LED billboard time from about 100 to 600 BDT per minute, with premium junctions at the higher end. Your written quote confirms the current rate for the screens you choose.

What changes the price

FactorWhy it matters
Location and facingBusy junctions, such as Sylhet, Bondor Bazar, cost more than quieter roads.
Slots per hourMore plays per hour means more people see your ad, and a higher price.
Campaign lengthLonger bookings are usually priced better per day than short bursts.
Number of screensA package across Sylhet and nearby areas is priced by its screens, not as one screen.
CreativeBringing a finished video or image costs nothing extra; design and animation are quoted separately.

Static billboards

Printed static billboards are priced differently: rent per square foot per month, plus printing and installation. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts regular billboard rent from about 500 to 3,000 BDT per square foot, depending on location and size.
