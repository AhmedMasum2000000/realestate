Metro Rail LED Advertising

Metro Rail LED advertising puts your video ad on the digital screens inside Dhaka Metro Rail coaches, where commuters ride the same route every day. It suits brands that want repeated, seated attention from office workers, students and families travelling between Uttara and Motijheel.

Questions answered

What is metro rail LED advertising?

Video ads played on the digital screens inside metro coaches in Dhaka, seen by passengers during their ride.

Which metro line can I advertise on?

Dhaka Metro Rail MRT Line-6, which runs between Uttara and Motijheel. Current screen coverage is confirmed when you enquire.

How long should a metro ad be?

Keep it short, usually 10 to 15 seconds, and make it work without sound: passengers often watch without audio.

Can I combine metro with LED billboards?

Yes. Pairing in-coach screens with roadside LED billboards near stations such as Farmgate, Karwan Bazar and Mirpur-10 repeats your message on the same journey.
