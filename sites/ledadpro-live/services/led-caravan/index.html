LED Caravan (Mobile LED Screen Van)

An LED caravan is a vehicle carrying a large LED screen that brings your ad to the audience: it drives planned routes, parks at markets, campuses and events, and plays video with sound. It reaches places fixed billboards cannot, from Dhaka neighbourhoods to district towns.

Who it reaches

  • Shoppers at markets and bazaars
  • Students at campuses and colleges
  • Crowds at fairs, events and product launches
  • Towns and districts without LED billboards

Questions answered

What is an LED caravan?

A van or truck fitted with a large outdoor LED screen and sound, used to show ads on the move or at parked stops.

Where can an LED caravan go?

Anywhere the vehicle can drive and park: Dhaka areas, markets, campuses, event grounds and district towns, subject to local rules.

Can it play sound?

Yes. Unlike most roadside billboards, a caravan can play video with sound at stops.

What is it best for?

Launches, roadshows, sales campaigns, activations and reaching towns without fixed LED screens.
