LED screen rental in Bangladesh

LED AD PRO rents LED screens and LED video walls for events, product launches, concerts, conferences and fairs in Dhaka and across Bangladesh. The rental includes delivery, setup, testing, an operator during the event and take-down. For a screen that moves between locations, see the LED caravan.

What you can rent

  • Indoor LED video walls for stages, conferences and exhibitions
  • Outdoor LED screens for concerts, fairs and launches
  • LED caravan: a mobile LED screen van for roadshows and district tours
  • Custom LED displays for shops and showrooms

How it works

  1. Tell us the event

    Date, venue, indoor or outdoor, audience size and what will play on the screen.

  2. Get a screen proposal

    We suggest the screen size and layout for the venue and viewing distance, with a written quote.

  3. Setup and testing

    Our crew delivers, builds and tests the screen before your event starts.

  4. Run the show

    An operator plays your content during the event and handles changes on the day.

  5. Take-down

    We dismantle and remove the screen after the event.

Questions answered

How much does it cost to rent an LED screen in Bangladesh?

LED screen rental is priced per day by screen size, indoor or outdoor use, setup and the operator. Send the date, venue and the size you have in mind for a written quote.

What size LED screen do I need?

It depends on how far the audience sits from the screen and the size of the venue. Tell us the venue and audience size and we propose a size and layout.

Do you provide outdoor LED screens?

Yes: outdoor screens for concerts, fairs and launches, and the LED caravan, a vehicle with a large screen that can move between locations.

Is an operator included?

Yes. Delivery, setup, testing, an operator during the event and take-down are part of the rental.

Can you rent a screen outside Dhaka?

Yes. Tell us the city and venue; transport is included in the quote.
