About LED AD PRO

LED AD PRO is a digital out-of-home advertising company in Bangladesh, based in Gulshan 1, Dhaka. We sell airtime on 56 LED billboard screens in 21 areas, rent LED screens for events, run the LED caravan and sell Metro Rail LED advertising. LED AD PRO is the official LED partner to AD Pro, whose team has delivered campaigns for 400+ brands.
