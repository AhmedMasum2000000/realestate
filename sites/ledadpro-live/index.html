        Questions, answered

        How much does LED billboard advertising cost in Bangladesh?

        LED billboard time is priced by screen, slots per hour and dates. LED AD PRO’s published guide puts it at about 100 to 600 BDT per minute, with premium Dhaka junctions at the higher end. Static billboards are rented by size, at about 500 to 3,000 BDT per square foot. See the full pricing guide.

        Where are your LED billboards?

        In 21 areas: Dhaka (Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Mirpur, Mohakhali, Airport, Farmgate and more) and cities including Chattogram, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Cox’s Bazar, Bogura, Cumilla and Feni. Every screen is listed with a photo on the locations page.

        How quickly can my ad go live?

        Usually within days of confirming screens, dates and artwork in writing. There is nothing to print or install on an LED screen.

        What do I need to send for an LED billboard ad?

        A short video (MP4) or still image (JPG or PNG) made to each screen’s pixel size. We send the exact size, orientation and ad length with your quote. We can also design the ad for you.

        Do you rent LED screens for events?

        Yes: LED screens and video walls for events, launches and concerts, and an LED caravan that takes a screen on the road. See LED screen rental.

        How do I book?

        Call +880 1958-503753 or email info@ledadpro.com with your brand, areas, dates and budget. You get a shortlist of screens, availability and a written quote.
