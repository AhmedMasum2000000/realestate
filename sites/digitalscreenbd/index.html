Our approach.

A simple rule:
the right junction beats a big budget.

We plan around where your buyers already are. A screen on Gulshan Circle-2 at 6 pm, a board on the airport road, four screens round Mirpur-10: each one chosen for who passes it, not for how it looks on a map.

Then we make it work. Creative cut to each screen size, a written schedule, and proof photos when it is live. Attention and performance; you need both.
