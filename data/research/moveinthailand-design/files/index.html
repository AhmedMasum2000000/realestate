A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

The things worth knowing before you take the next step.

I’m still researching. Is it too early to talk?+

That is a good time to start. You do not need a selected visa or a property shortlist. A first conversation helps put your options, timeline and next decisions in order.

Can you help with both my visa and my home?+

Yes. We coordinate the visa questions and your housing search together, so the lease, location and arrival timeline make sense for the same move.

How will I know what I’m paying for?+

Before paid work starts, ask for a written scope and fee breakdown. Official fees, our service fee and any third-party costs should appear separately. Changes need a revised quote.

Can you guarantee a visa approval?+

No. The embassy, BOI or immigration authority makes the decision. Our role is to explain the route, organise the evidence and help you understand what remains uncertain.

Do I need to buy property to move to Thailand?+

Buying a home is not the starting point for every move. Many people begin with a rental. Visa permission and property ownership are separate questions that deserve separate checks.

What happens after I arrive?+

We can coordinate the next steps for housing and settling in, then discuss Residency Care for the renewals and reporting relevant to your status. The scope is agreed around what you need.
