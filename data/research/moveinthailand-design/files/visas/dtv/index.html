DESTINATION THAILAND VISA

Work remotely. Make room for a different rhythm.

If your work travels with you, the Destination Thailand Visa may be a route to explore. Start with your purpose, your financial evidence, and the Thai embassy responsible for your application.

Who should explore the DTV?

Overseas remote workers, freelancers and people joining eligible activities such as Muay Thai or Thai cooking can explore this route. Qualifying spouses and dependent children may also have a route. A hobby, a course booking or a remote job title alone does not establish eligibility.

Put the evidence before the application

Bring together proof of your current location, your finances and your reason for visiting. Work contracts, a portfolio or activity documentation may be relevant. The embassy handling your application sets its document requirements and may ask for more evidence.

Make the stay match the purpose

Five-year visa validity is different from permission to stay for five years continuously. The DTV does not provide permission to work for Thai companies or freelance for Thai clients. Tell us how you actually work so your shortlist reflects your circumstances.

A practical next step

We can organise a document checklist, identify questions for your embassy and coordinate a rental search around your intended stay. Ask for a written service quote before authorising application support.

A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

The things worth knowing before you take the next step.

Does the DTV let me work for a Thai employer?+

It is not a Thai employment route. Explore the appropriate employment visa and work permission if you intend to work for a Thai company.

Is the financial threshold a fee?+

No. Evidence of funds is different from an application fee. Confirm acceptable evidence and the official fee with your responsible embassy.

Does five-year validity mean I can remain for five years without leaving?+

No. Visa validity and the permission granted for an individual stay are separate. Read the current entry and extension conditions with your responsible authority.

Should I book an activity before checking the evidence?+

First confirm that the activity and provider documentation meet your embassy’s current requirements. Understand the provider’s cancellation terms before making a payment.

Can my family use my DTV plan?+

Qualifying spouses and dependants may have a route, with their own evidence. Review the relationship documents and each family member’s intended activity before applying.
