What the brochures leave out

Do enough deals in Pattaya and the same things keep going wrong

01 Foreign freehold is a quota, not a right.

Only 49% of a building's saleable area can be foreign-owned freehold. Ask whether the quota is free on that unit before you fall in love with the view.

02 A chanote is not the same as a Nor Sor 3 Gor.

Both get called a title deed. Only one is fully surveyed and GPS-marked. The difference shows up when you try to sell, not when you buy.

03 A foreigner cannot own land. Anyone who says otherwise is selling you a problem.

Leasehold and company structures are legitimate and common. Nominee shareholders are not. That distinction is worth understanding before you sign.

04 Thirty years is the lease. The renewal is a promise.

A 30+30+30 lease is 30 years registered and 60 years of goodwill. Price it as thirty and treat the rest as upside.

05 Ask who pays the transfer fee before you agree the price.

Transfer fee, specific business tax and withholding tax can move the real number by six figures. 'Split 50/50' means nothing until it is written down.

06 A sea view from the bathroom is not a sea view.

If you have to lean out to see it, the next buyer will notice too. View premiums are the first thing to evaporate on resale.

07 A guaranteed rental return is a discount you have already paid for.

The guarantee is usually priced into the purchase. Work out the yield on the unit without the scheme, then decide.

08 Check the sinking fund before you check the gym.

A building with no reserves is a building with a special assessment coming. Ask for the juristic person's accounts. A good one will hand them over.

09 Low season is the honest season.

Anything will rent in January. If a landlord shows you occupancy figures, ask for May through September.

10 The cheapest unit in a good building beats the best unit in a bad one.

You can renovate a kitchen. You cannot renovate a juristic person, a car park ratio or a neighbouring construction site.

11 Off-plan is a bet on the developer, not on the building.

Look at what they finished five years ago and whether they finished it on time. The show unit tells you nothing.

12 If the price has not moved in a year, the price is not the problem.

It is usually the title, the quota, the access or the neighbour. Find out which before you negotiate.
