Sites: ledadpro.com, adprobd.com, iraveyou.online, bpop.bd, dhakaad.com.
Sources: a live crawl of each site (home + inner pages, robots, sitemaps, headers) and
Search Console data for the last 16 months. Competitor SERP checks were not possible

---

## 0. The issue that affects all the LED sites: one business, many look-alike domains

The VPS hosts 30+ domains for the same outdoor-advertising business (ledadpro.com,
adprobd.com, billboardadpro.com, digitaladpro.com, gulshanled.com, …). Several share the
same phone numbers (+880 1958-503753, +880 1316-743003) and describe the same screen
network with different counts (56 screens on ledadpro.com, 58 on adprobd.com). Some are
empty placeholders (bdledad.com, dhakaled.com and ledadhub.com have only the domain as
their title).

Google treats a group of near-identical sites built to catch the same searches as
**doorway sites**. It usually ranks one of them and filters the rest, and it can demote
the whole group. This is the main reason none of these sites will climb, whatever the
on-page work.

**Fix (strategic, highest impact):**
1. Choose **ledadpro.com** as the single main site. It has the most history (489 clicks
   and 23.6K impressions in 16 months, average position 11.8) and the best build.
2. 301-redirect the LED domains that have no distinct purpose to the matching
   ledadpro.com page (for example gulshanled.com → /locations/gulshan/, and
   adprobd.com/billboards-chattogram.html → /locations/chattogram/). This passes on
   whatever links and history they have.
3. Keep a separate site only where it serves a different audience or brand, with its own
   content and no copied screen lists.
4. Use one screen count, one address and one phone set everywhere, so Google and AI
   engines see one consistent entity (NAP consistency).

### Technical
- **http:// and https://www. both answer 200 instead of redirecting** to
  https://ledadpro.com/. That creates duplicate versions of every page. Fix: one 301
  rule in .htaccess.
- **Old WordPress pages are still indexed** alongside the new site: wp-sitemap.xml lists
  90 URLs. Review them, redirect the useful ones to their new equivalents, and noindex
  or remove the rest.
- The home page meta description is 174 characters, so Google cuts it off. Trim it to
  about 150.
- The home page H1 reads "Light upGulshanLED AD PRO…" because of the animated word.
  Keep the animation, but give the H1 one clean phrase, for example "LED billboard
  advertising in Dhaka and across Bangladesh".
- 2 home-page images have no alt text.
- Every page shares one og:image. Use the area's photo on each location page so links
  shared on WhatsApp or Facebook show the screen.

### AEO / GEO / AI Overviews
- Location pages already have FAQPage, Service, Place and Breadcrumb schema, and there's
  an llms.txt. That's good.
- **The home page has no FAQ.** Add 5–6 direct Q&As ("How much does LED billboard
  advertising cost in Dhaka?", "How long is a slot?", "What file format do I send?")
  with FAQPage schema. AI Overviews draw from exactly this kind of short, factual
  answer.
- Put real numbers on the pages (price ranges per area, slot length, plays per day,
  screen sizes). AI answers quote specific figures and skip vague text.
- Add Organization `sameAs` links (Facebook page, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile)
  once the Business Profile has been moved to almasum5495.

### CRO
- The phone and WhatsApp contacts are there. Add a short **"Get a quote" form**
  (name, phone, area, dates), and put a sticky WhatsApp button on mobile.
- Show trust signals above the fold: brands you've run campaigns for, the number of
  campaigns, and one case-study photo.

## 3. iraveyou.online — music (ACND & Apurba)

Strong build: Person, MusicRecording, MusicAlbum, MusicEvent and FAQPage schema, 1,210
words on the home page, a canonical tag, and a 28-URL sitemap. Search Console has only
just started collecting data, so there's no history yet.

**Gaps:**
- **http:// answers 200 instead of redirecting to https**, creating duplicates. Fix with
  one .htaccess rule.
- No llms.txt. Add one describing the artists, releases and links (a GEO gain:
  ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude use these files).
- The "Listen" page is thin (89 words). Add release notes, credits and dates.
- The response header has no charset (the page's own meta tag covers it, but sending
  `charset=utf-8` is safer for the Bangla text).
- For entity/knowledge-panel signals, link the artist pages to Spotify, Apple Music,
  YouTube, SoundCloud, Discogs and MusicBrainz through `sameAs`, and create or claim
  the MusicBrainz and Wikidata entries.
- CRO: add a newsletter or booking contact. Right now there's no way to get in touch.

## 4. bpop.bd — B-POP Tags (hosted on Cloudflare, not the VPS)

The biggest gaps of the five:
- **Only one indexable URL.** The whole site is one page with #anchor links, and several
  links are empty (`href=""`). Google can only rank one page for everything.
  **Fix:** give each episode, guest and "personality" its own URL (/episodes/slug/)
  with a title, description and transcript or summary.
- **No sitemap** (404), no canonical tag, no structured data at all.
- 6 of 7 images have no alt text.
- **AI crawlers are blocked.** Cloudflare's "block AI bots" setting puts GPTBot,
  ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, GoogleOther, Applebot-Extended and others in robots.txt.
  That keeps the site out of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Apple Intelligence answers,
  which is the opposite of GEO. **Fix:** in Cloudflare → AI Crawl Control, allow the
  search and answer bots. You can still keep `ai-train=no` if you don't want the content
  used for training.
- www.bpop.bd answers 200 instead of redirecting.
- The title shows "B-POP Tags â Off the Grid": the dash is an encoding problem
  (`Content-Type: text/html` has no charset). Add `charset=utf-8`, or replace the
  dash.
- Schema to add: Organization, WebSite, and PodcastSeries/PodcastEpisode (or VideoObject
  if the episodes are videos).
